The subtle rule that a rewrite could break.

A $150 member basket gets an $11.25 discount. Its discounted subtotal is $138.75, below the $150 free-shipping threshold. The baseline charges $7.95 shipping. Turn on the pricing regression in Shadow mode to catch a candidate that silently changes this rule.
